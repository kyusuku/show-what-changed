- Source snapshots preserve staged, unstaged, untracked, ignored dependency, index, and HEAD state. Unsupported layouts, conversion filters, stale plans, source changes, size bounds, and unsafe paths fail before unsafe execution.

- Terminal cancellation, output failures, and concurrent cleanup preserve consistent public results, manifests, and lock ownership. Encoder recovery does not rewrite capture, provenance, or coverage results.

## T15 repeatability

The exact installed C2 archive passed its final Linux repeatability check in two independent runs. Fourteen masked source-frame comparisons had a maximum changed-pixel ratio of `0.000177734375`, below `0.001`. Sixteen decoded boundary frames had a maximum mean RGB difference of `0.01821398202137998` between runs and `2.036029114026563` against their composed source images, both below `3`. The result and individual measurements are in `evidence/artifacts/linux-final-02/repeatability/report.json`. The gate compares two independent fixture runs using the masked source PNGs and decoded segment-boundary video frames. It does not use MP4 byte equality as the repeatability criterion.

```sh
node scripts/repeatability.mjs /absolute/path/show-what-changed-0.1.0.tgz /absolute/new-repeatability-directory
```

The required bounds are a source-frame changed-pixel ratio no greater than 0.001 and decoded-video mean RGB error below 3. The report identifies the candidate digest, two successful comparison commands, individual frame results, and retained fixture identity. Their output envelopes and media evidence are retained in the same directory. The immutable Linux gate reports `releaseGateComplete: true`. N6 is complete.
